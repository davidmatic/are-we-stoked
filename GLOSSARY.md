# Are We Stoked

A deliberately simple, text-only page that tells you whether Utah and Eastern Sierra ski resorts are getting snow, and how excited to be about it.

## Language

**Resort**:
A ski area on the page's fixed list (Alta, Snowbird, Brighton, Solitude, Park City, Deer Valley, Snowbasin, Powder Mountain, Mammoth, June), each with its own verdict. Named as the ski area, never the nearby town; "Park City" means the Park City Mountain resort, not the town; "Mammoth" means Mammoth Mountain and "June" means June Mountain.
_Avoid_: Location, city, mountain, spot, "Park City Mountain", "Ogden", "Mammoth Mountain", "Mammoth Lakes", "June Mountain", "June Lake"

**Area**:
A group of Resorts close enough to share weather (Cottonwood Canyons, Wasatch Back, Ogden Valley, Eastern Sierra), shown together on the page.
_Avoid_: Region, canyon, zone

**Stoke**:
How excited the page is about a Resort's snow; more snow means more stoke.
_Avoid_: Hype, excitement level, rating

**Get stoked**:
The Stoke for snow that is forecast to fall at a Resort but hasn't fallen yet; it gains an extra "o" as the forecast amount grows.
_Avoid_: Getting stoked, Stoked (for forecast snow), incoming, predicted

**Stoked**:
The Stoke for snow that has actually fallen at a Resort. Reserved for when the page learns about fallen snow.
_Avoid_: Get stoked (for fallen snow), fresh, pow day

**Stoke stake**:
The page read like a snow stake: every Stoke starts in the same column and each "o" is a fixed mark, so day to day only the length of the Stoke changes, never the layout around it.
_Avoid_: Meter, gauge, bar, scale

**Not stoked**:
The verdict for a Resort with too little snow coming to get stoked about.
_Avoid_: No snow, dry
