# Are We Stoked

A deliberately simple, text-only page that tells you whether Utah ski resorts are getting snow, and how excited to be about it.

## Language

**Resort**:
A ski area on the page's fixed list (Alta, Snowbird, Brighton, Solitude, Park City, Deer Valley, Snowbasin, Powder Mountain), each with its own verdict. Named as the ski area, never the nearby town; "Park City" means the Park City Mountain resort, not the town.
_Avoid_: Location, city, mountain, spot, "Park City Mountain", "Ogden"

**Area**:
A group of Resorts close enough to share weather (Cottonwood Canyons, Wasatch Back, Ogden Valley), shown together on the page.
_Avoid_: Region, canyon, zone

**Stoke**:
How excited the page is about a Resort's snow; more snow means more stoke.
_Avoid_: Hype, excitement level, rating

**Getting stoked**:
The Stoke for snow that is forecast to fall at a Resort but hasn't fallen yet; it gains an extra "o" as the forecast amount grows.
_Avoid_: Stoked (for forecast snow), incoming, predicted

**Stoked**:
The Stoke for snow that has actually fallen at a Resort. Reserved for when the page learns about fallen snow.
_Avoid_: Getting stoked (for fallen snow), fresh, pow day

**Not stoked**:
The verdict for a Resort with too little snow coming to be worth getting stoked about.
_Avoid_: No snow, dry
